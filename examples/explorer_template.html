01A distribution over structure

P(T | A) ∝ ∏(i,j) ∈ T Aij

The Bayesian spanning forest of Duan & Roy represents clusters as rooted trees. An auxiliary root connects those trees into a weighted spanning tree with kernel A. Sampling the structure exposes alternatives that one selected partition cannot show.

The model and its derivation

02Exactness has a scope

Wilson's algorithm draws a forest exactly conditional on its kernel scales. Fixed-scale draws are independent; adaptive scales use a Gibbs chain. The displayed probabilities are either conditional closed forms or estimates from retained draws, as marked in the selected view. Changing λ selects a separately computed run.

03Geometry is a different question

The reference graphs show neighborhood connectivity and a minimum spanning tree. A spectral view changes the display coordinates, not the fitted posterior. These are reference experiments for studying geometry; no manifold-recovery or new-method claim is implied.
